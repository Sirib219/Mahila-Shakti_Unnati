Project Overview

Mahila-Shakti Unnati is designed to support self-help group and micro-finance workflows. It replaces scattered notebook entries and informal message records with a simple Android app that stores member, savings, loan, repayment, income, expense, and report data locally.

The project focuses on role-based access, local database storage, admin approval, group coordination, financial tracking, and export-ready reporting.

Problem Statement

Many SHG and micro-finance records are maintained manually, which can lead to missing data, unclear repayment status, and difficulty preparing reports. This app helps organize those records digitally and makes review easier for members and coordinators.

Who Can Use This App?

Individual Users

Can track personal income, expenses, savings, budget limits, and basic financial records.

SHG Members

Can join a self-help group, record savings contributions, view loans, and follow repayment activity.

Group Coordinators

Can manage SHG members, create loans, track repayment progress, record savings, and export reports.

Admin

Can approve or reject user accounts and SHG membership requests before access is given.

Internship Evaluators

Can review the project structure, working APK, source code, screenshots, features, and documentation.

Community Groups

Can understand how a simple mobile app can support micro-finance record keeping and accountability.

How The App Works

1

Register

User registers as Individual, SHG Member, or Group Coordinator with required details.

2

Admin Approval

Admin reviews new accounts and approves valid users before app access.

3

Manage Records

Users record savings, income, expenses, loans, repayments, members, and budgets.

4

Export Reports

Filtered reports and loan histories can be exported as CSV files for sharing or review.

Key Features

Role-Based Login

Separate flows for Admin, Individual users, SHG Members, and Group Coordinators.

Member Management

Stores member details, group names, roles, phone numbers, and saving goals.

Savings Tracking

Records savings contributions by member and group.

Loan Management

Creates loans with borrower, amount, interest, purpose, due date, and repayment status.

Reports

Filters income and expense records by type, category, date range, and amount.

CSV Export

Exports filtered reports and loan repayment history through Android sharing.
