Mahila-Shakti Unnati

Android micro-finance support app for women, SHG members, and group coordinators.

GitHub Repository Demo Video

What This Project Does

Mahila-Shakti Unnati helps digitize self-help group and micro-finance workflows. Users can register, get admin approval, manage SHG members, record savings, create loans, track repayments, filter reports, and export CSV records.

Key Features

Registration & LoginUsers can register by role and log in after admin approval.
Admin ApprovalAdmin can review and approve users and SHG member requests.
SHG MembersCoordinators can manage member records and saving goals.
Savings TrackingRecords member-wise savings contributions for SHG groups.
Loan ManagementCreates loans, tracks repayment progress, and shows pending amounts.
Reports & CSV ExportFilters financial records and exports CSV reports through Android sharing.
